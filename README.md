# Object Tracker Support
Welcome to the support page for **Object Tracker** — a privacy-first iOS app that helps you recognize, organize, and maintain the objects that matter to you. Use your camera to add items to your personal collection and identify objects you have already saved. Keep photos, documents, notes, and maintenance details linked to each item, all within a **private local database on your device**. Object Tracker also lets you create relationships between objects—for example, connecting components to a machine, accessories to a vehicle, or devices to a room. It gives you one convenient place to understand what you own, access its related documentation, and keep track of its maintenance.

## Frequently asked questions
What can I use Object Tracker for?
You can use Object Tracker to manage household items, tools, appliances, electronic devices, equipment, collections, and other personal belongings. It is also useful for objects made up of related components.

How do I add an object?
Use the camera to capture or recognize an item, then save its information in your personal collection. You can add details, photos, documents, and maintenance information to each object.

Can I recognize an object I have already saved?
Yes. When you point the camera at an object, Object Tracker can help you identify whether it is already present in your database and retrieve its saved information. This feature is currently in beta and may not work as expected. If so, please use the standard search function instead.

Can I attach documents and images to an object?
Yes. You can associate photos, documents, and useful information with each saved object, so important material remains connected to the item it belongs to.

Can objects be connected to one another?
Yes. Object Tracker supports parent–child relationships. For example, you can connect a device to a room, components to a machine, or accessories to a vehicle.

Where is my data stored?
Object Tracker uses a **private local database on your device** to manage your objects and their related information.
